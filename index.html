03 / THE GARMENT

Made like a label.

STADIA° is a clothing label first. The cut, fabric and fit are equal to the meaning of the graphic.

Designed to sit naturally within a wardrobe, each shirt is celebrated in appearance and meaning. Every detail is considered, from the weight of the fabric to the placement of the coordinates.

01 / Proportion
A clean silhouette and carefully judged placement keep the design proud and balanced.
02 / Hand-feel
Selected for its weight, softness and drape, with colour that remains rich over time.
03 / Finish
Crisp printing and controlled detail, finished to withstand repeated wear.

04 / ETHOS

Wear where you belong.

It is the starting point.

Our standard is simple: the shirt should feel as good as the place means.

01

Place before branding

The place is the identity. We use its location, name, the emotion and the memories attached to it.

02

Restraint over noise

Co-ordinates and a place name. Nothing oversized, nothing unnecessary, nothing competing for attention.

03

Fewer, better pieces

We favour affordable quality garments and controlled design.
